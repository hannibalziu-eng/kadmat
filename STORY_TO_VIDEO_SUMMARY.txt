🎯 WORKFLOW:

STEP 1: Story Analysis (2-3 seconds)
        ↓ Analyze text with AI
        ↓ Extract main scenes
        ↓ Generate visual descriptions

STEP 2: Image Generation (1-2 minutes)
        ↓ Create prompts for each scene
        ↓ Generate images with AI
        ↓ Support multiple art styles

STEP 3: Voice Generation (10-20 seconds)
        ↓ Convert story to voice
        ↓ Choose language & voice
        ↓ Customize speed

STEP 4: Video Creation (2-5 minutes)
        ↓ Combine images with audio
        ↓ Add background music
        ↓ Apply transitions
        ↓ Export as MP4

RESULT: Professional video (50-200 MB)
